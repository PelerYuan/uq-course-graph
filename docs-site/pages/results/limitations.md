# Limitations

- The UQ site can change its HTML structure or available years.
- JavaScript-rendered requirements pages require Chrome and `chromote`.
- A flattened edge table cannot fully encode Boolean alternatives; consult `prereq_logic.csv`.
- External nodes show dependencies outside the selected plan, not missing data.
- Published prerequisites do not guarantee course availability, timetable compatibility, or degree completion eligibility.
- Free-text academic rules require human review.
- Statistics based on keyword matching or flattened alternatives are descriptive approximations.
